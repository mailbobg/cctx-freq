# R7 preregistration: quarterly cash-and-carry — no outcome yet

2026-10-04. New return mechanism after Relative30's cost and persistence failure: hold a matched-base spot long and linear fixed-expiry futures short. The payer is the demand for dated leveraged upside; the premium can converge by expiry. This differs from F1's directional funding sorts, the old funding-only cash-and-carry toy (which ignored basis/collateral), and R5's cross-venue perpetual funding spread. A premium is not guaranteed net income: terminal spot execution/index mismatch, widening basis, separated collateral and execution costs can destroy the trade.

Primary evidence and platform boundary, checked before price screening:

- Binance's [USDT-margined delivery contract description](https://www.binance.com/en-IN/support/faq/detail/3ae441db4ae740e19af3fe9228eb6619) describes linear, stablecoin-collateralized BTC/ETH contracts with fixed quarterly expiries, last Friday08UTC, and possible gaps before listing. The [public-data repository](https://github.com/binance/binance-public-data) describes official monthly/daily archives and checksum validation. Contract existence must be validated with actual archive rows and listing metadata; no spliced continuous contract replaces expired symbols.
- [BIS Crypto carry](https://www.bis.org/publ/work1087.htm) supplies an economic motivation and identifies margin/liquidation risks. Its historical summary return is not evidence for this version's return or collateral yield.
- Local `Exchange.market_is_future` currently requires `type == swap` and a linear market. Fixed-expiry futures are rejected. Existing paired-service infrastructure could support a separately reviewed coordinator; there is no claim this trial is already a usable Freqtrade class. Do not modify the exchange filter or activate anything before profitability/execution validation.

Frozen research version:

- Only BTCUSDT and ETHUSDT spot plus their **individual** USDT-margined quarterly delivery contracts. No altcoin substitution, borrowing, collateral yield or contract leverage above1x. Starting total equity10,000 USDT:5,000 spot cash and5,000 futures cash. Spot holdings cannot automatically supply futures margin. Both futures shorts share the futures cash; spot and futures balances are recombined only while the portfolio is flat at scheduled entries. No modeled transfer of an unrealized spot gain to rescue a short.
- Same R6 windows:2021-02-01–2023-12-31 and2024-01-01–2026-08-31. They are reused history. First decision is the Monday window start; later decisions are the Monday following the previous quarterly expiry (actual last Friday of March/June/September/December). Select the nearest future quarterly expiry, not the cheapest or best hindsight contract. No daily entry search. If that contract was not listed/traded at the prior Sunday, the asset remains cash for that cycle and the absent listing is recorded. Gaps while actively held are unreviewed input and block assessment.
- From completed prior Sunday spot/future prices, enter an asset only when `future/spot -1 >0.0048` (the fixed four-fill0.12% cost hurdle on matched base notional). This nominal hurdle only avoids obviously uneconomic entry; it does not guarantee12% account yield or cover future price-dependent exit costs. No horizon, hurdle, date or symbol sweep. If one asset is inactive, its allocation stays cash rather than doubling the other.
- Allocate half the spot/futures sleeve budgets to each asset (25% of total equity on each nominal side before price differences). Buy and short exactly the same base quantity, limited jointly by available spot cash and futures1x collateral **after fees**, with a1% reserve and entry gross<=99% total marked equity. Shorts never credit sale proceeds to usable cash. Qty is fixed for the cycle; no daily rebalance or stop tuning.
- Entry at each asset's Monday traded OPEN after the closed Sunday signal. Close both price legs at the Thursday daily CLOSE preceding Friday08UTC expiry, then remain cash until Monday; avoid claiming perfect settlement execution at an index. A window end closes all holdings at its final daily CLOSE even before scheduled expiry. Charge0.12% on each actual bought/sold leg, including all initial/final closes; stress0.24%. This is last-price screening with explicit all-in costs, not atomic real fills.
- Daily equity includes spot marked value and signed futures unrealized P&L; report each sleeve balance and remaining futures collateral. **No perpetual funding** is added to a verified delivery contract. Daily traded high envelope provides a conservative unresolved margin concern at assumed0.5% maintenance; it is not exact historical mark liquidation. Price-only screening may reject a failed return gate. A possible pass requires true contract mark prices, finer candles, dated contract/fee/listing records and actual two-leg execution/collateral reconciliation before risk is certified.
- Candidate is the equal-allocation two-asset rule. BTC-only and ETH-only are diagnostic controls with identical one-asset collateral constraints, not automatically adopted winners. No additional variant or weighted ensemble follows a failure.

Verify partial-archive versus genuine active gaps, expired-symbol identity, exact hedge quantities, spot cash debit, absent short proceeds, reductions/closing P&L, sleeve isolation, Sunday/Monday causality and final ledger reconciliation before any outcomes. A data-source failure is retained and repaired with real sources; never zero-fill an active gap or replace a contract using future availability.

Reject the first complete version if either window has CAGR<=12%, daily MTM DD>5%, nonpositive aggregate double-cost return, or unresolved active data/margin problems. Only a complete pass can proceed toward a new independent forward cohort. Keep all failures; no running bot or live account changes.
