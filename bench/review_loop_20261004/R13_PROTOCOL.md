Protection is only one added mechanism. Use TA-Lib Wilder ATR(22) on completed DAILY HIGH/LOW/CLOSE, unstable-period0. Fixed3 multiplier and22 bars reference the published default Chandelier parameters: https://chartschool.stockcharts.com/table-of-contents/technical-indicators-and-overlays/technical-overlays/chandelier-exit . This trial uses explicitly position-anchored, ratcheted levels rather than the chart indicator's unrestricted pre-entry rolling high; these changes prevent a fresh entry from inheriting an unrelated pre-entry price peak. They are fixed now, not tuned after outcomes.

At a fresh Monday entry, arm max(0, actual modeled entry OPEN -3*prior-day ATR22). On later daily00UTC boundaries, a held asset's level becomes max(previous armed level, highest HIGH of the last at-most22 completed daily candles during this still-open position -3*prior-day ATR22,0). The entry day can contribute its HIGH only after it completes. A partial trim/add never resets its entry day or lowers its stop. A full closure clears state; a genuinely new Monday position initializes anew. Unknown/nonfinite ATR or required daily prices fails input validation, not an invented volatility or fill.

At each minute OPEN, first sell an existing position if the preceding completed minute's LOW breached the level armed during that preceding minute, or if the current OPEN is at/below the now-armed level. A completed-minute touch sells at the **following minute's actual OPEN**, including recovery or further gap, plus the stated costs. No fill at the theoretical stop, no within-bar high/low ordering assumption, no trailing from the current day's future HIGH. The last minute's touch is pending until the following day00 OPEN; newly updated daily levels cannot be applied retrospectively to the preceding minute. A gap-triggered opening sale precedes ordinary weekly sizing. If it occurs on Monday, that asset cannot re-enter that same Monday. There are no additions after Monday00, no discretionary stop override and no reallocation to the companion after midweek stops. The companion's ordinary Monday sizing is unchanged. Only final requested CLOSE may liquidate a still-pending position at the common terminal fee/price.
