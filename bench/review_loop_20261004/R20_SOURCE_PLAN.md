Three distinct information approaches:

1. **Bitcoin aggregate realized cost basis (recommended feasibility probe).** Bitcoin UTXO realized capitalization values coins at their last on-chain movement prices; dividing by native supply could provide a network cost basis rather than another moving average of market price. A market below that basis may indicate broad underwater inventory and stressed trend participation. The natural accounting boundary is equality, not a fitted MVRV multiple. This mechanism is still a hypothesis: last movement is not a purchase, self-transfers/miner/new-coin/lost-coin accounting and historic fiat reference rates matter, and a BTC proxy may not govern ETH. Verify exact primary definition/asset support, original decimals and **historical public versions** before recommending a rule. No metric-to-return claim yet.
2. **Stablecoin monetary supply.** Issuance/redemption could describe available crypto liquidity, but treasury inventory, minting without circulation, cross-chain migrations and wrapped/native token duplication make gross supply different from capital flow into BTC. Current tickers/aggregate totals cannot be assumed complete at past dates. Inspect actual asset/metric coverage and scope before any aggregate or sign/horizon choice.
3. **Native transaction fees/activity or mining health.** Fees can indicate block-space demand, but also stress/self-transfer/spam; ETH protocol/L2/fee-accounting changes break a simple cross-asset proxy. Mining difficulty is consensus-specific and not a comparable post-Merge ETH signal; miner block timestamps do not guarantee public-receipt times. Defer this family unless a concrete metric/source/knowledge clock is actually feasible.

Primary entry points: publisher's https://github.com/coinmetrics/data (daily community CSV, mutable schema/asset coverage), https://docs.coinmetrics.io/api/v4/ (ordinary public community API and field/status semantics), https://github.com/coinmetrics/docs-website/blob/master/asset-metrics/asset-metrics-overview.md (daily aggregation and reviewed/revised fields), https://github.com/Blockstream/esplora/blob/master/API.md (native block identity/header, not an aggregate metric or historic broadcast clock).
