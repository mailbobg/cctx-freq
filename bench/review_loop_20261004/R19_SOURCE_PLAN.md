# R19: cross-market risk information feasibility

Source stage only, frozen before collecting new numerical series or candidate returns. All prior rejected families and adoption gates remain unchanged. This is a new information entrance, not a cutoff/lookback rescue of funding, flow, stop or short-direction trials.

Three approaches considered:

1. **SPX option-implied volatility term structure (recommended source probe).** A higher 30-day than 3-month expectation could indicate near-term common risk stress before a weekly crypto position is renewed. This is a risk-information hypothesis, not evidence that U.S. option prices predict crypto. Natural boundary is equality, without choosing a VIX level or fitting a period. Cboe defines the two option-implied horizons at https://www.cboe.com/tradable_products/vix/term_structure/ . Investigate VIXCLS and VXVCLS archived vintages at ALFRED, whose public documentation explicitly describes past-date observations: https://alfred.stlouisfed.org/help/downloaddata .
2. **U.S. equity trend confirmation.** SP500 is a daily closing price index, with only ten years on FRED and no dividends (https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/SP500). It could observe common risk appetite but overlaps the existing price-trend information and entails another horizon. Defer rather than evaluate many horizons.
3. **Dollar/rates/liquidity macro regime.** Economically plausible financing channel, but release/vintage clocks, revisions and a concrete sign/horizon would require a separate protocol. It is not a source-faithful signal simply because current FRED levels exist. Defer; do not select whichever proxy returns the best result.

First inspect real ordinary public responses for VIX and three-month VIX. Retain status, original bytes, SHA, requested series/vintage/range, exact local receipt and any errors. No credentials, paid access, hidden-state inspection, retrieved-code execution or access workaround. Direct Cboe current historical CSVs provide a counterpart, not historic knowledge proof. VIX3M file URL is a discovery lead until its actual official response/schema is validated; third-party descriptions are not evidence for values or timing. The primary ALFRED series identity/source/frequency and download semantics must corroborate it.

Probe paired VIXCLS/VXVCLS at three fixed Saturday vintages: 2021-01-30 (first early Monday), 2024-01-06 (first full recent week), 2026-08-29 (last recent Monday). Request the preceding fourteen calendar days. Check returned headers identify **each requested series and exact vintage**, observation dates, missing codes, decimal values, paired dates, duplicate/order errors and values dated after the vintage. If a vintage query is ignored, blocked or unidentifiable, it is an error, not current-history substitution. Current Cboe values can expose discrepancies but must not overwrite an archived value.

ALFRED release/vintage dates have no exact UTC release clock. Do not interpret observation labels as available at that day's crypto UTC OPEN. A potential Monday-only experiment would use the preceding **Saturday vintage**, available conservatively at vintage-date UTC midnight plus **two days**, allowing a whole date-only release to finish across time zones. Pair only the same nonmissing observation date in both series, retain its U.S. trading-date identity, and do not carry a value older than seven calendar days or treat a partial latest pair as a safe earlier state. Weekend/holiday closure is distinct from unknown/missing values. Friday 16:15 America/New_York conversion (DST-aware) is only an observation-completion lower bound; it cannot replace archival vintage availability. The two dates/clocks remain explicit. Tests must cover DST, holidays, unequal series dates, future vintages, missing latest rows and equality/native-decimal boundaries.

Critic: SPX implied volatility already reflects expectations and risk premia, markets can decouple, a weekly exit can lag or miss rebounds, and current reconstructed history may differ from information actually archived. A term inversion is not a guaranteed crash forecast, a VIX futures trade, or an executable volatility hedge. Do not conflate option-implied index and future settlement. If archival identity and access are insufficient, stop this source stage without P&L or invented historical availability. If feasible, freeze exactly one candidate rule, execution and control protocol **before** full vintage acquisition/backtesting. No bot/class/cohort changes at this stage.
