## Risk audit, frozen before new outcomes

Reconstruct cash and BTC/ETH units from every exported fill. Include initial capital, pre-fill equity at native minute OPEN, each sell/buy and its fees, post-fill OPEN and native CLOSE. Fees at initial entry and terminal liquidation participate in drawdown. For long inventory during a minute also compute an OHLC envelope: cash + sum(units * per-asset high) as an upper equity bound and cash + sum(units * per-asset low) as a lower equity bound. The assets' highs/lows need not occur simultaneously, so the envelope is a conservative bound, not an observed synchronous portfolio price. Do not impose an intraminute high-before-low order as a measured event. Compare each lower bound to the cumulative possible upper peak including its own minute to bound possible drawdown; actual OPEN/CLOSE events provide a sampled drawdown. Account for cash/unit changes at the known minute-open fill boundary.

Report sampled OPEN/CLOSE maximum drawdown, conservative OHLC-envelope drawdown, peak/trough dates and equity, first 5% and 15% sampled breaches (descriptive thresholds only), fees, minimum cash/units, terminal accounting gaps, daily parity and missing/zero-activity held minutes. Complete intraday coverage is false if any required held minute/source is missing or a daily/fill parity check fails. Incomplete coverage does not imply zero return or a losing strategy; the observed sampled risk is a lower-bound diagnosis, not a complete certificate. Even complete archive coverage does not certify tick liquidity, bid/ask fills, continuous risk or independent forward profitability.

Meaningful deterministic tests must catch lost entry/exit fees, incorrect sell fees/cost double count, omitted initial peaks, simultaneous-high assumptions, minute gaps/duplicates/unit errors and order-price mismatch. Replay from frozen native bytes with HTTP disabled and require byte-identical derived audit outputs. Preserve baseline results and all pre-existing source/output/root/config hashes. No grid, no cutoff tuning and no new adoption on a pending user reply.
