# Holdout test 2021-01 → 2023-12 (pre-registration)

Date: 2026-09-26. Written before any 2021–2023 data is downloaded or looked at. Append-only result log at
the bottom; nothing above the log may be edited after the first result.

## Why

Every conclusion so far (bench, X9–X11, the 20-round loop) comes from 2024-01 → 2026-09. That window has
been looked at dozens of times; it no longer tests anything. 2021–2023 has never been loaded here. It holds a
bull market (2021), a bear market with two collapses (LUNA 2022-05, FTX 2022-11) and a quiet year (2023).
This file uses it exactly once, on the strategies we already hold, with no parameter changes.

## Data

- Source: data.binance.vision monthly spot archives (includes pairs delisted since), converted to
  freqtrade feather in `user_data/data_holdout/binance/`. Script: `user_data/bench/fetch_vision.py`.
- 5m candles from 2020-10-01 (warm-up) to 2023-12-31; 15m/1h/4h/1d resampled from 5m.
- Symbol reuse (e.g. LUNA old → LUNA new in 2022-05): if a pair has a gap > 3 days and the price across the
  gap changes by more than ×2 or ÷2, keep only the data before the gap. An open trade on a pair whose data
  stops is then closed by freqtrade at the last price it had, so a collapse is booked as a loss.

## Universe (point in time, no survivorship)

For each year Y in 2021, 2022, 2023: the 120 Binance USDT spot pairs with the highest quote volume over
Q4 of Y−1, among pairs with data on at least 60 days of that quarter. Excluded: stablecoin and fiat bases
(USDC BUSD TUSD PAX USDP DAI FDUSD UST USTC SUSD EUR GBP AUD), leveraged tokens (base ends in UP, DOWN,
BULL, BEAR). Pairs freqtrade cannot map to a market even with `allow_inactive` are dropped and counted.

Each year is a separate backtest with that year's universe, starting from 10,000 USDT. A position still
open on 31 Dec is force-closed (a "bag reset" for X8; recorded).

## Acceptance criteria (same as the loop; all must hold)

1. Compounded return over 2021–2023 > 0.
2. Each calendar year > 0.
3. Profit with the best 3 months removed > 0.
4. Bootstrap 95% CI of total profit (all three years' trades pooled) excludes 0.
5. Mark-to-market max drawdown ≤ 25% in every year.
6. Fee 0.2% per side: compounded return still > 0.

Reported but not a criterion: equal-weight buy-and-hold of the same universe, BTC hold, average profit per
trade vs the 2024–26 figure (edge decay), trade count per year.

X11 effect passes if the 24h excess return over BTC of the flushed coins is > 0.2% with t > 2 over all
episodes, and positive in at least 2 of the 3 years.

## What each outcome decides (fixed now)

- E0V1ENPF pass → only candidate for a small live account to measure real fill cost. Fail → its dry-run
  continues only as a slippage measurement; not a live candidate.
- Elliotv8PF: informs the same decision for the dip-buy family (both pass = family result is stronger).
- X8PF pass → dry-run continues as a candidate. Fail → dry-run continues only as a forward record.
- TSMOM30 pass → write a separate spec for a BTC+ETH trend dry-run. Fail → trend line closed.
- X11 effect pass → X12 (flush rebound with per-episode exposure capped) may be specified, and its
  backtest must be judged on 2024–26 only, since the holdout is then used up. Fail → flush line closed.
- Nothing is re-run on 2021–2023 with changed parameters. After this file's log is written, 2021–2023 is
  ordinary in-sample data.
