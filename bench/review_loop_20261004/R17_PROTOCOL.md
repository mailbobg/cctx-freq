## Fixed rule

- Universe: BTC, ETH, SOL, XRP, BNB, ADA, DOGE, AVAX, all native spot /USDT and same-native-unit USDT perpetuals on Binance. No asset replacement, multiplier token, borrow, staking/reward income, idle-cash interest or hidden leverage. All must have verified availability before their first decision; missing source is unreviewed, not automatically excluded.
- Decisions every Monday01:00 UTC, with a fixed one-hour availability allowance for rates settled in the scheduled hour. Forecast cutoff is Monday00:00. Normalize only documented sub-second archive offsets to that hour; preserve the exact original timestamps. Forecast is the exact decimal sum of actual rates in(cutoff-7days,cutoff], not mean-per-settlement times an assumed count. Declared interval changes must reconcile with adjacent actual settlement gaps; unknown/repeated/gapped calendars fail closed.
- Qualify only forecast **strictly above0.0058**: four fills at the frozen0.0012 all-in side cost give0.0048, plus0.0010 basis allowance, on one leg's notional. Choose the two highest qualifying sums, exact ties by symbol. No predicted/next rate, current Monday funding after00, price signal or future realized income determines choice. Two fixed slots; one qualifying name uses one slot with the other in cash. No redistributed spare slot.
- Hold until the next Monday01. At each weekly boundary close all existing paired positions at that hour's actual spot/perpetual trade OPEN, account for both exit costs, and only then pool realized cash and split it equally between spot and USDT futures collateral. Reopen selected pairs using the same hour's trade OPEN and both entry costs. Full weekly closes intentionally realize collateral before repooling; no free unrealized spot margin or netted-away round-trip fees. The7-day income hurdle pays for this explicit weekly execution. No off-Monday trading, order-timing/holding sweep, cash transfer while positions remain, or leverage to meet the return gate.
- Native quantity per selected slot uses half of each cash sleeve (therefore roughly25% total account notional in spot and25% in the matching short), bounded by both actual trade prices/fees. Preserve1% reserve. A further joint scale must keep short gross at <=99% of post-fill marked futures equity and total post-fill gross <=99% of marked total equity. A single slot does not consume the unused second slot. Long spot units exactly equal negative perpetual units. Separate futures cash/P&L/fees/actual funding and spot cash/units are always visible.
- Actual settled funding payment equals short native quantity times associated mark times settled rate. It is not starting/current notional times an average rate. Rates at a scheduled hour precede that hour's modeled orders on the old quantity, including a possible Monday01 settlement. No funding on the initial flat book. Capture raw API pages/hash/length/time and verify against original native CSV timestamps/rates/intervals. Missing held associated marks are explicit, not filled with previous/current CLOSE. An OPEN-mark approximation with fixed adverse/favorable HIGH/LOW sensitivity may be reported only as an uncertified screen; it cannot establish source/execution fidelity or adoption.
- Ring-fenced futures cash plus marked short UPNL must remain solvent throughout holding. Use observed hourly marks and a disclosed0.5% maintenance screen, plus an adverse future-HIGH envelope, to flag concerns. Neither spot gains nor positive total account equity erase a futures margin concern. A concern rejects the necessary screen; no invented liquidation fill/recovery or permission to borrow collateral. Exact exchange historical tiers remain a later fidelity requirement.
